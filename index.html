Messages (Annual & Bi-Annual Editions)

Optional. Each message you fill in is printed on its own page, in this order, at the start of the annual and bi-annual newsletters, and listed in their Table of Contents. Leave a message empty and it is simply left out. They are not used in monthly issues.

Annual & Bi-Annual Editions

Combine the months of a full year, or of one half-year, into one newsletter (with the leadership messages and a Table of Contents) or one consolidated Excel workbook. Upcoming events and conferences are left out of these editions — they appear in monthly issues only.

Start a New Year (Erase Data)

Erases every submission — events, publications, conference presentations, blogs, editorials, guest editorials, puzzles and upcoming items — so the portal starts fresh. Your settings (mission, vision, quote, contacts, logo, cover photo) and the admin password are kept. Generate your annual newsletter and Excel report first, and download a backup. This cannot be undone.
